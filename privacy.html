Privacy notice

Oksanabeauty is an independent beauty studio operated by Oksana Ilnitskaya. This website introduces the treatments and explains how to contact the studio. It has no online booking form, customer account, payment checkout, newsletter sign-up or in-house analytics.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who is responsible?

This website is operated by Oksana Ilnitskaya under the business name Oksanabeauty. Studio address: Vormerlaan 7, 2288 GC Rijswijk, room 1.14, the Netherlands. For privacy questions, use the contact details in section 8.

2. Information you choose to share

The website does not ask you to enter personal details, and it does not send messages to an Oksanabeauty website database. You may contact the studio yourself by phone, WhatsApp, Instagram or email. If you do, Oksana will use the information in your message only as reasonably needed to reply to you and discuss or arrange the service you asked about. Please do not send unnecessary or sensitive information.

3. Hosting and technical information

When you visit the website, the hosting provider and network services may automatically process technical request information, such as your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested, and basic browser or device information. This can be necessary to deliver, maintain and secure the website. Oksanabeauty does not use this technical information to identify visitors for advertising or keep a customer database through the website. The hosting provider handles its own technical logs under its terms and privacy policy.

4. External resources

The website currently loads fonts from Google Fonts, photographs hosted by Unsplash, and an embedded Google Maps map. When these resources load, your browser connects directly to the relevant provider and may share your IP address and technical browser information. Google and Unsplash process such requests under their own terms and privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy and Unsplash Privacy Policy. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Gmail and Google Maps also lead to services governed by their own policies. Oksanabeauty does not control how those providers process information.

5. Cookies and language preference

This website does not use its own advertising cookies or analytics tools. It saves your selected language in your browser storage (localStorage) so the same language can be selected on your next visit. This preference stays in your browser and is not sent to Oksanabeauty by the website. The embedded map and other third-party resources may use their own cookies or similar technologies; their handling is governed by the relevant provider’s privacy policy.

6. Messages and retention

The website itself does not collect enquiry details into a salon database. If you choose to contact the studio, your message may remain in the email, phone or account you used and in the studio’s corresponding inbox or account, so Oksana can respond and handle your enquiry or appointment. Those services may also keep technical records or backups under their own settings and retention rules. Messages are not used by Oksanabeauty to build advertising profiles or send newsletters. Information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the conversation or service, unless a longer period is required by law.

7. Your privacy rights

Where applicable under data protection law, you may ask whether Oksanabeauty holds personal information about you and request access, correction or deletion, or ask to restrict or object to its use. Contact Oksana using the details in section 8. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Some rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

8. Contact and changes to this notice

For questions about this notice or a privacy request, contact Oksana at oksanabeauty1984@gmail.com. If website features change—for example, if an online booking form, analytics or additional third-party services are added—this notice should be updated to reflect those changes.
